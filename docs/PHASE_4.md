# Phase 4 — Modern Military Base Map

Create a compact, original modern military base designed for Team Deathmatch.

Layout:
- Three primary combat lanes with secondary routes and flank paths
- Close-quarters interiors, medium-range courtyards, and limited long-range sightlines
- Accessible buildings, stairs, platforms, and controlled verticality
- Balanced cover for attacking, defending, and repositioning
- Distinct team spawn regions and navigation connections

Implement:
- Modular environment geometry and appropriate collision meshes
- Readable landmarks and clear navigation
- Tactical cover placement
- Limited destruction of designated barriers and objects
- Physics and navigation updates when destructible objects change the environment
- Basic lighting and material variation

Avoid excessively open sightlines, unavoidable spawn traps, and obstructed routes. Use original or properly licensed assets.

Validate player collision, accessible routes, spawn areas, and performance. The map must be playable, not merely a static visual scene.
